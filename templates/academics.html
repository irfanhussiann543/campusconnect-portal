Choice Based Credit System (CBCS)

At CampusConnect, our students are not restricted to rigid learning paths. Through CBCS, learners customize their curriculum according to their passion.

  • Open Electives: CSE students can take robotics, EV propulsion, or architectural design courses.
  • Minor Degrees: Earn a major in Mechanical Engineering with an officially accredited minor in Artificial Intelligence.
  • Industry Fast-Track Semester: 8th semester dedicated entirely to full-time corporate internships without campus class obligations.
