About Our Institution

A legacy of intellectual curiosity, world-class pedagogical methods, and engineering impact since 2002.

Institution Overview

Nurturing Leaders for the Technological Era

CampusConnect Institute of Technology was founded with a singular aspiration: to engineer solutions for society's most intricate challenges by harmonizing rigorous academic standards with creative exploration.

Sprawling across a 75-acre lush green, eco-sustainable campus in the heart of the tech corridor, CampusConnect serves over 12,500 aspiring innovators. We take pride in our autonomous status, which allows us to dynamically revitalize our syllabus every year in collaboration with top corporate partners such as Google, Microsoft, and Cisco.

Dr. Arthur Vance, Ph.D.
Chancellor & Chief Academic Administrator

"We do not merely teach engineering; we cultivate minds capable of architecting a cleaner, smarter, and more empathetic world."

Our Vision

To be a globally admired center of holistic technological higher education and translational research, producing visionary engineers committed to societal progress and sustainable industrial innovation.

  • Pioneering interdisciplinary problem-solving paradigms.
  • Instilling profound human ethics and responsible technological development.
  • Leading green-campus and sustainability initiatives across all domains.

Our Mission

To provide student-centric, experiential learning grounded in fundamentals, powered by advanced infrastructure, and enriched by continuous industrial co-creation and entrepreneurial incubation.

  • Modernize curricula constantly with emerging industry technologies.
  • Facilitate world-class research publications, patents, and startups.
  • Ensure equitable access to technical education through generous scholarships.
Journey of Growth

Milestones in Our History

A quick retrospective of how CampusConnect became one of the country's most sought-after technical institutions.

2002
Foundation Stone

Established with 3 engineering branches and 180 students under the visionary patronage of academic philanthropists.

2012
Autonomous Accreditation

Granted autonomous status by UGC/AICTE; launched CBCS curriculum and the Central Digital Library complex.

2020
AI Innovation Center

Inauguration of the dedicated AI & Data Science wing featuring NVIDIA DGX compute clusters and tech incubation labs.

2026
NAAC 'A++' Grade

Awarded the highest national accreditation score of 3.72/4.0 and ranked among the Top 30 technical colleges in NIRF.

Campus Amenities

World-Class Infrastructure & Facilities

Providing students with an environment where learning, research, athletics, and cultural passions flourish symbiotically.

Central Digital Library

Home to over 110,000 physical volumes, 15,000+ IEEE/ACM digital journals, high-speed RFID automated kiosks, and 24/7 silent study lounges.

Advanced R&D Laboratories

Over 50 specialized engineering labs equipped with NVIDIA GPU servers, Keysight RF test suites, Cadence VLSI EDA, and 3D printing CNC centers.

Smart Interactive Classrooms

Acoustically treated multimedia lecture halls equipped with 4K touch display panels, lecture recording cameras, and high-speed campus Wi-Fi 6.

Olympic Sports Complex

Features an international standard 400m synthetic running track, floodlit football turf, indoor basketball arena, badminton courts, and modern gymnasiums.

Modern Hostels & Dining

Separate air-conditioned and non-AC residential blocks for boys and girls, with biometric security, solar heating, multi-cuisine cafeterias, and laundry.

Startup Incubation Hub

A 15,000 sq.ft co-working hub offering seed funding, legal mentoring, intellectual property support, and pre-incubation grants to student founders.
