How to Apply

Step-by-Step Admission Process

A transparent, merit-guided pathway to securing your seat at CampusConnect.

1
Online Application

Fill in your academic credentials, 10th & 12th marks, and branch preferences via our portal.

2
Merit Screening

Qualifications

Eligibility Criteria

B.Tech 4-Year Undergraduate Program
  • Qualifying Exam: Passed 10+2 (Higher Secondary) with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM).
  • Minimum Marks: Aggregate minimum of 60% in PCM (50% for reserved categories).
  • Accepted Entrance Exams: JEE Main, KCET, COMEDK, or CampusConnect Aptitude Test.
  • Age Requirement: Candidates must have attained 17 years of age as of Dec 31, 2026.
M.Tech 2-Year Postgraduate Program
  • Qualifying Degree: B.E. / B.Tech in relevant engineering discipline with min 60% CGPA.
  • Selection: Valid GATE score receives direct interview admission; non-GATE through university written test.
